Core Rules for Personal Data Protection

1. Passphrase Over Passwords: Use 4 random words strung together (e.g., correct-horse-battery-staple).

2. Eliminate Cleartext: Never keep passwords in phone notes or plain text files.

3. Enforce MFA/TOTP: Always prefer authenticator apps (Google/Microsoft Authenticator) over SMS OTPs.
